The morning at the Adventurer's Guild is always a madhouse.

The morning for the receptionists at the Adventurer's Guild is always busy.

"-Hey, you dumb bitch! How many fucking times do I have to tell you to only bring quest forms that match your rank?!"

"-The reward for discovering a new dungeon will be paid out after we go through a separate verification process, so please be patient and wait."

"-Ah, you there! If you're going to fight, take it outside... you fucking bitches! Aaargh!! Guards!! Call the Guard Force!!"

It's just dizzying.

But even in a space as chaotic as this, there's a clear difference in atmosphere.

"Haaahm~"

"Heehee, my nails look pretty nice today."

Unlike the flock of female receptionists who look like they're fighting a war without swords.

The two male receptionists shamelessly sat at their desks with sleepy faces, yawning or tidying their nails.

With their vaguely pretty faces and frail, sorghum-stalk-like frames that made it hard to call them men, our delicate male receptionists were once again enjoying their leisure to the fullest while the female receptionists worked with veins popping on their necks.

"Ahem, aah~ ahem! Smith? Go get me a cup of water, will you?"

"Ah~ me too, me too."

And on top of that, they boss me around like fucking crazy.

Of course, the "Smith" those assholes are calling for is me.

"Ah, yes."

I, Seo Minsu, a K-youth in my 4th year adrift in this world, am struggling to survive as Smith of the Sahara tribe from a frontier desert region.

"Fucking bastards."

I went out the back door, spat 'Ptooey!' on the ground, then stopped by the storage to find an empty oak barrel and headed to the well.

"Oh, fucking sweet."

In this world where water is precious everywhere, you even have to pay for a bucket of water to wash yourself at an inn, so adventurers registered with the guild usually wash themselves at the guild-owned well.

Because of that, it's usually bustling with adventurers at all times except late at night, but for some reason, it was completely empty today.

"I swear to fucking god... one day I'm gonna stuff them in an oak barrel and throw them in the well."

This time, spent muttering to myself while diligently filling the oak barrel with the pulley bucket, is one of my few small pleasures these days.

I'd love to give those two bastards a knuckle punch to their heads right now—the kind that would make them yelp 'Oof!' and fold in half from a light 'thwack!'—but unfortunately, unlike me, who has no proper citizenship, those two are natives born in this city, so I don't dare mess with them.

"Hoo, this much is no problem now."

I easily lifted the oak barrel, filled to the brim except for about 1/3 of the space, used the back door, and expertly took the stairs to the 2nd floor, opening the kitchen door of the guild's dining hall and stepping inside.

"Hey~ I was just thinking it was about time you showed up— Pffha~!"

A charming woman with orange-tinted short hair and freckles around her nose that went well with her characteristic fierce smile. The Kitchen Manager, Big Sister Amela, grinned as she chugged a glass of beer.

"Still got nothing to do, I see, Big Sister."

"Huh? Nothing to do? You little asshole, then I'll eat this all by myself."

"Hehe, Big Sister. Your shoulders seem very tense."

At Big Sister Amela's action of showing off the well-roasted chunks of meat on the plate with that same fierce smile, I hastily put down the oak barrel and quickly ran to her side.

"This little asshole? When did I say you could fucking eat?"

"I'th gonna be eaten anyway..."

"Aaargh!! How many times have I told you not to talk with your mouth full, you asshole!!"

Though her words were harsh, at the sight of her silly grin, which was different from her words, I shut my mouth and gulped down the chunk of some unknown beast's meat I had stuffed in.

"Still busy, huh."

"Unless they're an asshole who wants to die, everyone wants to shove something in their stomach, so of course it's busy, you punk."

"You speak the absolute truth."

Like the 1st floor, the kitchen was also extremely busy.

The kitchen staff moved about hectically, doing knife work like mad or placing what were obviously heavy iron pans on the grill and shaking them up and down.

I looked at the only person in the kitchen who was at leisure: Big Sister.

"For lunch, I'd like a salad with plenty of meat, please."

"You crazy bastard. Why would meat go in a salad."

Leaving Big Sister, who was laughing in disbelief, behind me, I grabbed a round wooden tray and a few cups and went to the oak barrel I'd put down earlier.

With practiced hands, I poured a moderate amount of water into the cups, placed them on the wooden tray, and carried it down to the 1st floor.

"Haaack— Ptooey!"

Of course, before making the delivery, I didn't forget to add a special MSG for Susu-kkang A and B.

With all preparations complete, I slowly opened the back door of the reception desk with the wooden tray full of water glasses to work on my reputation within the guild today as well.

At the same time, a chill ran down my spine.

Cheap language and loud yelling should have been flowing out, but for some reason, not a single sound came from the half-open doorway, only the creak of the slightly old door I was holding.

Therefore, even if I didn't want to know, I knew.

That the one and only adventurer in my charge during my two months as a receptionist, and the only silver-rank adventurer in this city, was at the guild right now.

Damn it. I'm screwed.

Suddenly my mouth felt dry and my legs felt like they were trembling.

What should I call it? Maybe the fear from that time in my childhood when I bought a toy cockroach to scare my mom and was subsequently remodeled into a filial son via a physical backscratcher.

Anyway, the point is I'm fucking terrified.

But I had already opened the door halfway, and since I was in the pitiful situation of not being able to leave the city without the guild's guarantee, I had no choice but to open the door the rest of the way and go inside.

And just as I expected.

“......”

“......”

Our silver-rank adventurer Shiron, with her very charming red hair and slightly crooked, fierce eyes, was glaring at me from in front of my reception desk with her head tilted sharply to the side.

Gulp.

Regardless of my will, I kept swallowing dry saliva.

"You asshole. How long are you going to stand there like that."

"Yes? Ah, g-going now."

At her irresistible voice, I felt my mind going dizzy.

Forcing my staggering legs to move, I returned to my seat and first hastily put down the tray in my hands.

Thud!

"Uh......"

"What, you punk."

"N-Nothing."

I could only shut my mouth as I watched Shiron, of all people, pick up a water glass with my special MSG added and chug it down.

"Hmm, the water tastes a little sweet... You son of a bitch."

"Y-Yes?"

"Did you put something in the water?"

"T-T-That can't be. I-It's just water I drew from the well."

"......Is that so?"

As if she didn't trust my words, she glanced over the other water glasses, and Shiron's action made my blood run cold.

"S-So, what brings you here? You usually come in the evening, but today you've visited so early in the morning..."

"You asshole? So you're complaining that I came in the morning?"

"No, no. Of course not."

I succeeded in diverting her gaze from the water glass to me, but at the same time, my mind went blank.

Sergeant Jang. I'm only realizing it now, but you were a fucking nice guy.

Beautiful woman or not, this unreasonable situation just makes me want to tear up a little.

"You idiot. Enough. What other reason would I have for coming here? I finished the quest, so I came to report it."

Along with words packed with a feeling of patheticness, she threw a cloth pouch slightly larger than my fist onto the reception desk.

While watching Shiron's expression, I quickly untied the pouch and checked its contents.

Inside was a bundle of quest request forms stamped with the seal of the Caravan Master who had requested a caravan escort quest a fortnight ago.

It seemed she had brought them to the guild on behalf of everyone.

"G-Good work."

I smiled as naturally as I could, quickly opened the drawer below, grabbed the pouch containing the quest reward, and offered it to her very politely with both hands.

With a smack!, Shiron roughly snatched the pouch from my hands.

"Ahem, ahem. By the way, how about you check my contribution points?"

"......? Please wait a moment."

Seeing that her furrowed brow had leveled out, perhaps because she'd received the heavy money pouch, I quickly opened another drawer to check her contribution points.

"Let's see... 2450 points, plus today's quest makes... 2980 points..."

"What did you say, you son of a bitch?!"

With a CRACK!, the reception desk split halfway.

"Fuck!! That can't be!! You son of a bitch!! You must've miscalculated because you're an idiot!! Hey!! You there!!"

"Squeak?!"

An extremely agitated Shiron, her already red eyes now even redder, pointed at Susu-kkang B, and the guy answered as if having a seizure, looking like he was about to faint on the spot.

"You son of a bitch. You calculate it instead!!"

"Aaargh!! Y-Yes, I will......!!"

Even though he was hit in the face with the bundle of papers detailing the contribution points that Shiron threw, the guy just trembled and did as she ordered.

At the same time, I was suddenly terrified.

It's a one-in-a-million chance, but what if, just what if, I really did make a mistake?

Damn it. They say if a humanities major and a science major fall into this world, the humanities major has a higher chance of dying sooner, and it seems to be true.

"What's taking so long?!"

"I-I'm done!!"

"Yeah? So what's the score?"

"I-It's......2980..."

"Aaaaargh!!"

"Hieeeeeek!!"

Shiron screamed as if having a seizure, and at the same time, Susu-kkang B finally fainted, foaming at the mouth.

"What are you looking at, you bitches!! Move, fuuuuck!!"

At her cry, which was close to true madness, the adventurers parted to the left and right like the miracle of Moses, and through that path, Shiron kicked the guild's door with enough force to break it and stormed outside.

I really don't know why she's like that.

"Keck, keheheheuk."

I felt depressed about the split-in-half reception desk, but then I looked at the guy twitching on the floor, foaming at the mouth, and nodded my head in satisfaction.

Then, I picked up one of the water glasses that had miraculously remained safely on the tray and chugged it down.

Pfft...!!

Damn it. It was one of the glasses I spat in.
